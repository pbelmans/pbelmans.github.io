After seeing [John Christian Ottem, *Stable irrationality of quartic sixfolds*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10231)
on the arXiv yesterday,
I was reminded of my plans a little while ago
to make a website like [mgnbar.info](mgnbar.info)
surveying the state of the art on a precise question.
Inspiration for this I found in

- [Francesco Russo, *Rationality of cubic fourfolds via Trisecant Flops*](https://www.impan.pl/~pragacz/russo.pdf#page=4)
- [Olivier Debarre, *On rationality problems*](https://doi.org/10.4171/EMSS/83)

Without further ado, see [rationality.fanography.info](https://rationality.fanography.info).
It is just a single page,
with features very much like [mgnbar.info](mgnbar.info).
I'm not sure I like the methods overview,
maybe I will get rid of it at some point.
