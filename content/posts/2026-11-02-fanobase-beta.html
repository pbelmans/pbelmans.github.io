Together with several collaborators, we've just released a beta version of Fanobase: a database of Fano 4-folds constructed as zero loci on products of type A partial flag varieties.

type-a.fanobase.net

For more on what we are doing, see the announcement blogpost. This is very much work in progress: we will be releasing more examples and data on a regular basis, with updates on the Fanobase blog.

Have a look, and let us know if you have comments, corrections or suggestions!
