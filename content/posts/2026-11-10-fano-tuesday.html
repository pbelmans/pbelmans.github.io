We've started Fano Tuesday, a weekly series on the Fanobase blog. Each week, we'll discuss something interesting about one or more Fano fourfolds appearing in our project.

The first instalment is about Küchle's list and how it inspired Fanobase. In some sense, growing his list of 17 families to as many as possible is precisely one of the goals of Fanobase.
